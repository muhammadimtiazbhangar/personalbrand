Six OT detections, zero license cost

Every rule maps to MITRE ATT&CK for ICS or IEC 62443-3-3, and you can test it in the lab before it goes anywhere near production.

UC1 · Critical

Unauthorized Modbus write

Zeek logs + CDB allowlist: a write from any host that isn't an approved engineering workstation raises a level-12 alert.

UC2 · High

PLC project tampering

FIM with whodata on engineering files: see who changed the logic, when, and with which process.

UC3 · High

USB storage on HMI/EWS

Registry FIM builds a removable-media history for every HMI and engineering workstation.

UC4 · High

Off-hours remote access

Time-based rules on jump-host RDP sessions catch vendor and shared-account misuse.

UC5 · Medium

Hardening drift

A custom SCA policy scores every HMI against a baseline tagged with IEC 62443 SRs.

UC6 · Medium

Vulnerable OT software

Syscollector inventory + vulnerability detection, without touching the PLCs.

The reference architecture

Passive at Levels 0–1, agents only where the OEM approves, and the Wazuh server inside the OT zone.

L4–5 Enterprise SOC / SIEM ← alerts only, northbound via DMZ (one-way) L3.5 OT DMZ: jump host [agent] · OT firewalls [syslog] L3 Wazuh server (manager · indexer · dashboard) · historian [agent] L2 HMI [agent] · EWS [agent] · Zeek/Suricata sensor ← SPAN/TAP (read-only) L1 PLC · RTU · SIS: no agents, monitored passively L0 Sensors · actuators · drives

FAQ

Is the kit really free?

Yes. The rules and configs are MIT-licensed on GitHub, and the lab guide and mapping sheet are free to read, print and share.

Can I use this on a production plant?

Build and test it in a lab first. Before production, review it with operations and your OEM, and follow your management-of-change process. Never enable active response on Level 1–2 assets.

Which Wazuh version?

Written for Wazuh 4.14.x with Zeek JSON logs. Always confirm rules with wazuh-logtest on your version.

Is this an official Wazuh product?

No. It's a community resource by a Wazuh Ambassador and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Wazuh Inc.
